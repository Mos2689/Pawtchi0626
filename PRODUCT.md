# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Everyday dog owners, primarily urban and suburban adults, who treat their dog as family and want an easy record of daily care and shared life. The walk experience must work for first-time owners, multi-dog households, reactive dogs, senior dogs, people walking alone, and anyone using the phone one-handed outdoors with a leash in the other hand.

## Product Purpose

Pawtchi helps owners turn ordinary care into a sustainable habit and a meaningful history of life with their dog. As Pawtchi becomes walk-first, success means that starting a walk feels effortless, the phone can stay pocketed, and every completed walk returns a fresh, truthful memory without turning routine routes into a performance contest.

## Brand Personality

Warm, observant, and quietly confident. Pawtchi speaks with affection but not sentimentality, celebrates the dog's point of view, and makes everyday moments feel worth keeping without demanding attention.

## Anti-references

Pawtchi should not resemble a competitive running dashboard, a noisy social feed, a children's reward game, or a generic camera-filter app. Avoid pace anxiety, leaderboards, guilt, forced sharing, constant prompts, fake discovery, decorative glass effects, loud confetti, and UI that asks owners to stare at the phone while walking.

## Design Principles

1. Make the dog the protagonist. Routes, discoveries, memories, and language describe the dog's day.
2. Protect the walk itself. Core value accrues passively, and any interaction is optional, glanceable, and safe to defer.
3. Celebrate showing up, never speed. Routine, curiosity, and companionship matter more than distance or pace.
4. Make privacy tangible. Sensitive location and media remain private by default, with honest explanations of what is stored where.
5. Turn data into meaning. Pawtchi should synthesize route, context, and optional owner input into a memory that feels specific to this walk.
6. Earn sharing. A share artifact should be beautiful and meaningful enough to share without rewards or pressure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA where platform controls and contrast apply. Keep primary outdoor actions at least 48 points, readable in bright sunlight, usable one-handed, compatible with Dynamic Type and screen readers, and respectful of reduced-motion settings. Never disadvantage slow, small, senior, disabled, or reactive dogs. Prompts must be dismissible, avoid urgent motion, and never require interaction while the owner or dog is moving.
