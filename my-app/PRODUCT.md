# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Pet owners using Pawtchi during everyday care: checking personalised guidance, recording food and activity, understanding their pet's health, and keeping important profile details accurate. They need calm, immediately understandable controls that work in short mobile sessions.

## Product Purpose

Pawtchi turns a pet's profile, routines, food, health signals, and walks into practical personalised care guidance. Success means owners feel more confident about daily decisions and can understand what Pawtchi knows about their pet without needing specialist knowledge.

## Brand Personality

Warm, assured, and observant. Pawtchi should feel like a thoughtful companion with expert judgment: friendly without becoming childish, premium without becoming ornamental, and precise without sounding clinical.

## Anti-references

- Aggressive subscription banners, urgency, guilt, countdowns, or advertising inserted into routine product tasks.
- Generic medical dashboards that feel sterile, institutional, or alarmist.
- Decorative luxury tropes such as gradients, gold everywhere, glass effects, and status treatments that overpower the pet.
- Dense feature-comparison surfaces inside operational screens.

## Design Principles

- Keep the pet, not the product tier, as the central identity.
- Make important state visible at a glance, then reveal detail through familiar interactions.
- Sell confidence and better care outcomes, not a list of locked features.
- Use persistent but quiet monetisation that never interrupts profile management.
- Preserve trust through honest status, restrained copy, and predictable platform behaviour.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, clear labels, platform-familiar controls, and at least 44-point effective touch targets. Never rely on colour alone for status. Support screen readers with explicit state and action labels, respect reduced-motion settings, and keep copy understandable without subscription or veterinary jargon.
